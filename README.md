# Data projects

Data analysis and data science projects by Aman Chhetri, Master of Data Science student at Macquarie University.

| Project | What it answers | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| [IPL Match Analytics, 2008–2026](ipl-match-analytics) | What decides a T20 match, and how well can results be predicted before and during a game? | Python, SQL, scikit-learn, Matplotlib |
| [NSW Retail Sales Forecast, 2010–2025](nsw-retail-forecast) | Can NSW retail sales be forecast a year ahead, and which kinds of shops are hardest to predict? | Python, SQL, statsmodels, Matplotlib, pytest |
| [Sydney House Price Model, 2018](sydney-house-prices) | How well can a Sydney house's sale price be predicted from suburb, land size and zoning, and what happens when the market falls? | Python, SQL, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, pytest |
